# Privacy Policy — Halfcycle

**Version.** `2.0.0`
**Effective date.** 2026-09-24.

This policy explains how Halfcycle AI, Inc. ("Halfcycle", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares
information when you use our software, command-line tools, hosted services and websites (together,
"the Service").

## 1. Information we collect

- **Account information.** When you sign in, we receive information from your sign-in provider, such
  as your email address and an account identifier.
- **Information you provide.** Content you submit to the Service, such as answers, documents, uploads
  and messages.
- **Project and usage information.** Information generated when you use the Service, including
  information about your projects and development activity that our tools process to provide the
  Service, and records of the checks, steps and results produced along the way.
- **Technical information.** Information such as IP address, device and browser details, and logs of
  requests to the Service.
- **Cookies and analytics.** We and our analytics providers use cookies and similar technologies on
  our websites and web apps to understand how the Service is used. We use Microsoft Clarity for
  product and user analytics; Microsoft processes that data under its own terms — see the
  [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/privacystatement).

## 2. How we use information

We use information to provide, operate, maintain and improve the Service; to secure the Service and
prevent abuse; to measure usage and enforce limits; to communicate with you about the Service; and to
comply with legal obligations. We may create aggregated or de-identified information and use it for
any lawful purpose, including improving the Service.

## 3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with:

- **Service providers** who process it on our behalf — for example hosting, analytics, error
  monitoring and AI model providers — under their own terms and our agreements with them;
- **Other people you work with** on a project, where you share access to it;
- **Authorities or others** where required by law or to protect rights, safety and the Service;
- **A successor** in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets; and
- **Anyone else** with your consent.

## 4. Retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, and
afterwards as needed to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

## 5. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely
secure.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting us. Depending on
where you live, you may have further rights under local law, and we will respond to requests as that
law requires.

## 7. International processing

We and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries.

## 8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the updated version with a new effective date. If a change is
material, we will give notice — for example by asking you to review it when you next sign in. Continued
use of the Service after an update takes effect means you accept it.
